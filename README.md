## PROJECT SUMMARY:

The Inventory Tracker application is designed to help organize and manage inventory within a clothing warehouse. The app will allow warehouse employees to view available products, understand how they are classified, and identify the exact location of each item within the warehouse. By centralizing inventory information, the system will improve organization and reduce the time required to locate products.
This application focuses specifically on a clothing warehouse, where inventory management is more complex due to variations such as size, color, and category. Employees will be able to quickly access accurate inventory data, ensuring products are properly tracked and managed throughout the warehouse.
By streamlining inventory processes, the application will help employees work more efficiently and provide faster, more reliable service to customers. This is especially important for warehouses that supply products to multiple retail stores across the country, where accuracy and speed are essential.

The application solves the problem of inefficient and disorganized inventory management. Without a centralized system, employees may struggle to find products, update stock levels, or maintain accurate records, leading to delays and errors.
